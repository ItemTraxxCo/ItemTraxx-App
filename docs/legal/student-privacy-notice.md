# Part A: Student Privacy Notice

## 1. What ItemTraxx is

ItemTraxx is inventory-and-checkout software that schools and universities use to track equipment such as laptops, cameras, calculators, and lab gear, and to record who borrowed what and when. **Students do not sign in to ItemTraxx.** School staff run the checkout station and record a borrower's checkouts and returns for them.

## 2. What information we hold about students

The school decides what to enter. The information ItemTraxx can hold about a student (a "borrower") is limited to:

| Information | Details |
|---|---|
| **Borrower username** | By default a random codename (for example `TideFox481`) generated by ItemTraxx. A school administrator may instead enter or import their own value, up to 40 characters. |
| **Borrower ID** | By default a random ID (for example `4821KQ`). A school administrator may instead enter or import their own value. |
| **Access setting** | Whether any staff account can lend to the borrower, or only specific staff accounts. |
| **Checkout history** | Which items the borrower checked out and returned, when, and which staff account recorded it. |

ItemTraxx has **no fields** for a student's email address, phone number, home address, date of birth, grade, photo, grades, health information, discipline records, or location. We ask schools not to enter any of those anywhere in the system, including notes and imports.

**Codenames are pseudonyms, not anonymous.** The school keeps the roster that connects a codename to a student, and we treat the information as student records.

## 3. What we do not do

- We **do not sell** student information.
- We **do not use** student information for advertising or marketing, and we do not allow others to target advertising using it.
- We **do not build profiles** of students for any purpose other than the school's inventory management.
- We **do not share** student information with anyone except the school and the service providers that host and protect it for us (Section 5).
- We **do not use** student information to train generalized models.
- We **do not take or store photos or video**. If a school uses the camera on a staff device to scan barcodes, the camera image is used on the device only to read the barcode; it is not recorded, uploaded, or stored.

## 4. Why we use it

Only to provide ItemTraxx to the school: record checkouts and returns, show the school its inventory and audit history, keep the service secure and working, and help the school with support requests.

## 5. Who can see it

- **The school.** Its authorized staff see borrower records and history in their workspace. Staff accounts are separate from borrower records.
- **Service providers.** Student information is processed by **Supabase** (database and servers), **Cloudflare** (network security and delivery), and our backup storage provider. Our other providers (for analytics, support chat, and email) are set up **not** to receive student information. Replay recordings mask borrower usernames and IDs, and error reports contain no borrower data. Because staff could still accidentally type student information into a support chat or screenshot, we ask schools to avoid it. The complete provider list is in our [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md).
- **ItemTraxx staff.** A small number of authorized staff can access customer data only as needed for support, security, and operations, with logged access.
- **Others only as the law requires** (for example a valid court order), in which case we tell the school first where the law allows.

## 6. How long we keep it

- While the school uses ItemTraxx, borrower records and history stay in its workspace.
- If a school archives a borrower, we **permanently delete the record 365 days later**. The checkout history entries remain in the school's item history, but they are **no longer linked to that borrower**.
- If a school stops using ItemTraxx, its workspace is kept for a **30-day recovery period**, then **permanently deleted** by an automated process, along with its accounts and support records, and a deletion certificate is created.
- A school can ask us at any time to delete a student's information sooner (Part B, Section 7).
- Backups are overwritten on a rolling schedule of [[BACKUP_RETENTION_DAYS]] days.

## 7. How to see, correct, or delete a student's information

ItemTraxx holds student information **for the school**, so the school is the right place to ask. Parents and eligible students should contact the school's office or ItemTraxx administrator. The school can view, correct, or archive a borrower record directly in ItemTraxx. If you contact us, we will direct you to the school and help the school respond. Our contact: privacy@itemtraxx.com.

## 8. Security

ItemTraxx encrypts data in transit, separates every school's data from every other school's in the database, limits staff access, and monitors for abuse. Offline checkouts are stored encrypted on the staff device and expire within 24 hours. We will tell the school promptly if student information is affected by a security incident (Part B, Section 9).

## 9. Children under 13

Students do not use ItemTraxx directly. Where a school uses it with younger students, ItemTraxx relies on the school to authorize the collection of student information for educational purposes under the federal Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not ask a child for any information, and we delete information on the school's request.

## 10. Changes and questions

We will tell schools about material changes before they take effect. Questions: privacy@itemtraxx.com.

# Part B: FERPA / SOPIPA Compliance Addendum

This Addendum is between ItemTraxx and each school, school district, charter school, county office of education, college, university, or other educational institution that uses ItemTraxx (the "Institution"). "**Student Data**" means any information in an Institution's workspace that is directly related to a student, including borrower usernames, borrower IDs, and checkout history, whether or not it is pseudonymous. "**Student Data**" includes "education records" under FERPA, "pupil records" under California Education Code section 49073.1, and "covered information" under SOPIPA, to the extent applicable.

## 1. Roles and school-official status

1. **Institution control.** The Institution owns and controls Student Data. ItemTraxx processes it only on the Institution's behalf and instructions, and the Institution can access, correct, export, and delete it through the Service.
2. **FERPA.** For Institutions covered by the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (20 U.S.C. § 1232g; 34 C.F.R. Part 99), ItemTraxx is a **"school official" with a legitimate educational interest** under 34 C.F.R. § 99.31(a)(1)(i)(B). ItemTraxx (a) performs an institutional service or function (asset and equipment tracking) for which the Institution would otherwise use its own employees; (b) is under the Institution's **direct control** regarding the use and maintenance of Student Data; and (c) will use Student Data only for the purposes the Institution authorizes and will not redisclose it except as § 99.33 permits.
3. **Eligible students.** For colleges and universities, rights that FERPA gives parents belong to the student once the student turns 18 or attends a postsecondary institution. References to parents in this Addendum include eligible students.
4. **SOPIPA and California law.** For K-12 Institutions in California, ItemTraxx is an "operator" of an online service used for K-12 school purposes under the Student Online Personal Information Protection Act (California Business and Professions Code § 22584), and a "third party" receiving pupil records under California Education Code § 49073.1. ItemTraxx applies the same protections to postsecondary Institutions as a matter of policy.
5. **COPPA.** ItemTraxx does not collect personal information from children under 13 directly. The Institution authorizes the collection and use of Student Data for educational purposes in place of parental consent, as the Federal Trade Commission permits, and ItemTraxx uses it for no commercial purpose.

## 2. Use limits (SOPIPA covenants)

ItemTraxx will **not**: (a) use Student Data for **targeted advertising** on the Service or any other service; (b) use Student Data, including information we gather about the Service's use, to **build a profile** of a student, except for the Institution's authorized inventory and school purposes; (c) **sell** Student Data, except in a merger or acquisition as SOPIPA permits and subject to Section 8; or (d) **disclose** Student Data except to the Institution, to service providers that need it to provide the Service and are bound by terms at least as protective as this Addendum, as required by law, or in an emergency to protect health or safety as the law permits.

ItemTraxx may use **de-identified and aggregated** operational data about the Service (for example the number of records and checkouts per workspace, used to apply plan limits and operate the Service). We will not attempt to re-identify it, and we will not disclose it in a way that identifies the Institution's students.

## 4. Subprocessors

1. **Student Data processors.** Student Data is processed by Supabase (database, server functions, file storage), Cloudflare (network security, delivery, and logs), and cloud backup storage (Google Cloud). The current list is in the [Subprocessor Directory](subprocessor-directory.md) and the [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md).
2. **Other providers.** Our analytics, session-replay, support-chat, email, alerting, and paging providers are configured not to receive Student Data. They may incidentally receive limited information if a user places Student Data into a screenshot, support message, or chat. We instruct users not to, mask borrower fields in replay, and scrub telemetry, and we will delete any Student Data we find in those systems.
3. **Obligations and notice.** Each subprocessor with access to Student Data is bound by a written agreement that requires protections no less protective than this Addendum. We remain responsible for them. We will give the Institution at least **30 days' notice** by email to its administrators before adding or replacing a subprocessor that handles Student Data, and the Institution may object on reasonable data-protection grounds. If we cannot resolve the objection, the Institution may terminate the affected service and receive a refund of prepaid fees for the remaining term.

## 5. Security program

ItemTraxx maintains a written information security program that includes: encryption in transit (HTTPS with HSTS) and at rest by our hosting providers; tenant isolation enforced in the database by row-level security and service-only data-modification paths; role-based access; passwords of at least 12 characters, passkeys, two-factor authentication, and single sign-on; session management and revocation; step-up verification for privileged actions; encrypted offline storage with 24-hour pack expiry; rate limiting and bot protection; logging and audit trails of administrative and security-relevant actions; automated vulnerability scanning and periodic security testing; an incident-response process with automated alerting; backups; and a [Vulnerability Disclosure Policy](vulnerability-disclosure-policy.md). ItemTraxx limits employee access to Student Data to personnel who need it and who are bound by confidentiality obligations.

## 6. Parent and student access, review, and correction

The Institution is responsible for responding to parent and student requests. The Institution's administrators can view, correct, export, and archive borrower records and history in the Service. If ItemTraxx receives a request directly, we will not respond on the merits and will refer the requester to the Institution within **10 business days**, and we will help the Institution locate and provide, correct, or delete the data within **10 business days** of its request.

## 7. Retention, deletion, and return

1. **During the term**, borrower records stay in the Institution's workspace. When an Institution archives a borrower, ItemTraxx permanently deletes the record after **365 days** and unlinks it from the retained item history.
2. **On request.** On the Institution's written request (email to privacy@itemtraxx.com from a workspace administrator), ItemTraxx will permanently delete specific Student Data, or all Student Data, within **30 days**.
3. **At termination.** When the agreement ends or the Institution archives its workspace, ItemTraxx keeps the workspace for a **30-day recovery period** (during which the Institution may request a complete export), then permanently deletes all Student Data through an automated process and issues a **written deletion certificate** to the Institution on request. Backup copies are overwritten within [[BACKUP_RETENTION_DAYS]] days.
4. **Certification and enforcement.** ItemTraxx certifies that it will not retain, and will not make available to itself or any third party, Student Data after the deletion process completes, except (a) backups until they are overwritten, (b) pseudonymous security audit records that contain no student information (identifiers are random unique codes), and (c) as the law requires, in which case we will tell the Institution and limit the use. The Institution may ask for confirmation at any time. ItemTraxx will provide a written statement of compliance and, if requested, supporting evidence.

## 8. Change of control

If ItemTraxx is acquired or merges, the successor must honor this Addendum for Student Data already collected, or ItemTraxx must give the Institution notice and the chance to delete the data before the transfer takes effect.

## 9. Unauthorized disclosure or breach

If ItemTraxx confirms an unauthorized acquisition of or access to Student Data, it will notify the Institution's designated contact **without unreasonable delay and in any event within 72 hours of confirmation**, by email, and will include, to the extent known: what happened and when, the Student Data and the individuals' records involved, what we are doing to contain and remediate it, and what the Institution can do. We will keep the Institution updated, cooperate with its investigation and any notices to parents, students, regulators, or the California Attorney General that it or the law requires, and not notify individuals about an incident involving the Institution's Student Data without first coordinating with the Institution unless the law requires otherwise. These notices are in addition to the duty in California Civil Code § 1798.82, under which a business that maintains data it does not own must notify the owner immediately after discovery.

## 10. Institution responsibilities

The Institution will: (a) provide any notices to parents and students and obtain any consents the law requires for its use of ItemTraxx, and decide how it treats ItemTraxx in its annual FERPA notification and any directory-information policy; (b) use the generated codenames and IDs where it can, enter only the borrower information it needs, and **not enter** Social Security or government ID numbers, health information, grades or discipline records, photos, or other categories ItemTraxx is not designed to hold; (c) designate and manage its workspace administrators, use available security features, and promptly remove access for departing staff; (d) request deletion of Student Data it no longer needs; and (e) not use ItemTraxx directly with children under 13 (students do not sign in).

## 11. Audits and assistance

ItemTraxx will respond to reasonable written questionnaires and provide copies of its independent security reports (for example its ISO 27001 report) under confidentiality. On a reasonable request, no more than once a year unless there has been an incident, ItemTraxx will help the Institution assess compliance and will cooperate with any regulator's inquiry about Student Data.

## 12. Order of precedence and changes

This Addendum is part of the agreement between the Institution and ItemTraxx. If it conflicts with the Terms of Service on student data matters, this Addendum controls. A signed Data Processing Addendum or the California Student Data Privacy Agreement, where the Institution uses one, controls over this Addendum to the extent it is more protective. We will give the Institution at least 30 days' notice of any material change that reduces these protections, and a change does not apply to Student Data already collected without the Institution's consent.
